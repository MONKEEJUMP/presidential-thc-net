## Primary and official sources used for additions

### California Department of Cannabis Control regulations

Source: [Medicinal and Adult-Use Commercial Cannabis Regulations, revised January 1, 2026](https://cdn.cannabis.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2026/01/dcc_regulations_01012026.pdf)

Claims supported:

- California defines an infused pre-roll as a pre-roll into which cannabis concentrate other than kief has been incorporated or added.
- Licensed manufacturing protocols identify components, ingredient amounts, target cannabinoid concentrations, and production parameters; this supports the copy's emphasis on repeatable construction and batch-specific information.

The definition is used only to distinguish an infused pre-roll from a flower-only or kief-only roll. The article does not imply that every jurisdiction uses identical terminology.

### California Department of Cannabis Control labeling guidance

Source: [Manufactured cannabis products in final form — labeling checklist](https://www.cannabis.ca.gov/licensees/cannaconnect-compliance-hub/labeling/manufactured-final-form/)

Claims supported:

- manufactured cannabis labels identify product type, net contents, UID, batch or lot number, ingredients, and cannabinoid content;
- California permits infused pre-roll cannabinoid information as milligrams per package or as flower Total THC percentage plus added THC and CBD in milligrams; and
- the universal cannabis symbol is required on vape cartridge hardware at the specified minimum size.

The copy tells readers to inspect these fields; it does not claim this checklist is universal outside California.

### Federal tobacco definitions

Source: [27 CFR § 44.11, Electronic Code of Federal Regulations](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-44/subpart-B/section-44.11)

Claim supported: federal terminology defines a cigar as a roll of tobacco wrapped in leaf tobacco or another tobacco-containing substance. This is used to explain why traditional blunt language is associated with tobacco wrappers and why a hemp-wrapped cannabis blunt requires an explicit tobacco-free material distinction.

Editorial boundary: the article does not equate a cannabis blunt with a federally regulated cigar and makes no comparative health or safety claim.

### Primary cannabinoid-vaping experiment

Source: Kim et al., [Thermal transformation of CBD, CBDA, and Δ9-THC during e-cigarette vaping: Identification of conversion products by GC-MS](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40158472/) (2025).

Claim supported: coil power and cartridge-oil composition are experimental variables that change cannabinoid thermal transformation. The vape article uses this narrowly to explain why battery settings alter heating conditions and why a higher setting is not simply a faster version of a lower setting. It makes no health or effects claim from the study.

### Official California cartridge-disposal rule

Source: [California DCC — requirements for vape cartridge advertising, packaging, and labeling](https://www.cannabis.ca.gov/licensees/cannaconnect-compliance-hub/advertising-marketing-packaging-and-labeling/)

Claim supported: California requires messaging that spent cannabis cartridges go to a household hazardous-waste facility or another approved facility and prohibits packaging that implies they belong in household trash or recycling.

The article advises readers to follow the package and local waste authority because collection programs vary.
